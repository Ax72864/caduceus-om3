# Caduceus for OM-3 v0.17

- Everything in v0.16, plus the selected profile's name under the wheel on the Profiles page, from
  `OM3MOD/NAMES.BIN` (made by the Look Lab with `PROFILES.BIN`).
- Card files: `GUIDES.BIN` (page labels). Make `PROFILES.BIN` and `NAMES.BIN` in the Look Lab.
- Camera-tested.

Make it: run the launcher and type `v0.17` (or `python3 firmware/patch.py --release v0.17`). The files here are the patch (only the bytes Caduceus for OM-3 adds; no OM firmware), the page labels and the expected checksums.
