Profiles for the camera's Profiles page

Each of COLOR 1–4 has its own 5 profiles. On the camera's Profiles page, the 12 o'clock (Yellow) spot picks one: 0 is nothing (all neutral), 1–5 load a whole profile: every wheel plus Contrast, Sharpness, Shading and Shadows / Midtones / Highlights. Changes you make on the camera are kept in the selected profile. Drag the dots on a wheel to edit, and check the preview on the left. For the COLOR 1–4 profiles only: Monochrome profiles are not supported.

Save for the camera

OM3MOD folder

All 20 profiles go into PROFILES.BIN. It replaces the camera's bank, including profiles you changed or made on the camera, so to keep those, open the camera's PROFILES.BIN here first. After copying it over, every COLOR starts at 0, and your current camera settings stay as they are until you pick a profile.

Unzip and copy the OM3MOD folder to the top of the SD card, replacing the files. Needs Caduceus for OM-3 v0.16 or later (v0.17 or later shows the profile names). Older LOOKS.BIN and BUNDLES.BIN files can still be opened: their entries are added as profiles.

Import from Caduceus

Paste a look code or a pack link from the Caduceus Control Panel, or open a Caduceus pack (.json) or an E-P7 EP7MOD/LOOKS.BIN.

Adjusted for the OM-3: Hue, Light and Saturation, WB A/B and G/M, Blacks/Whites, Shadows/Midtones/Highlights, Contrast and Vignette (as Shading) carry over. Hue, Light and WB beyond ±5 are limited to ±5, the OM-3 wheel range. Caduceus-only features (noise filter, bloom, halation, clarity, Art Effects) and monochrome looks are left out, and the import says which.

Wheels run clockwise from Yellow at 12 o'clock, the same as the camera: Yellow, Orange, Orange-red, Red, Magenta, Violet, Blue, Blue-cyan, Cyan, Green-cyan, Green, Yellow-green. The dashed ring is 0; points outside it are +, inside are −. Drag a dot in or out, or select a slice and use the − / + buttons (or the arrow keys).

Hue + rotates a slice clockwise toward its next neighbour (Yellow → Orange, Blue → Blue-cyan). Light steps grow as they go out: ±3 is four times ±1.

WB / B·W: A/B at 12, Blacks at 3, G/M at 6, Whites at 9. A/B + is amber and − is blue; G/M + is green and − is magenta. Blacks + lifts (fades) and − crushes; Whites + brightens and − softens.

Color profiles only. Everything here is for Picture Mode COLOR 1–4. The Monochrome profiles are not changed and have no Profiles page.

Profiles page on the camera (INFO after WB/BW): only the 12 o'clock (Yellow) spot works. 0, or anything below 0, is nothing: everything neutral. 1–5 load that COLOR's profile 1–5; an empty number loads neutral, ready to build a new profile on the camera. Turning away from a profile keeps what it has, and a half-press saves the bank to the card. Exposure comp is shared by the whole mode, so it is a preview setting only.
